Metodologi Pemetaan Kuadran: Price Deviation & Duration of Price Movement

Basis Harga Wajar: MH-WMA (α = 0,5)
Price
Deviation
Sumbu Horizontal (X)
$$\left(\frac{P_t - \widehat{P}_t}{\widehat{P}_t}\right) \times 100\%$$
Keterangan:
$P_t$: harga aktual pada periode $t$
$\widehat{P}_t$: harga acuan MH-WMA periode $t$
Duration of Price
Movement
Sumbu Vertikal (Y)
$$\bar{d} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} DPM_i}{N}$$
Keterangan:
$DPM_i$: durasi pergerakan tiap episode (hari survei)
$N$: total pembalikan arah terhadap $\widehat{P}_t$
